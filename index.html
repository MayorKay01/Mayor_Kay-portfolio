A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity meets craft.

I’m Mayor_Kay, a frontend developer interested in how thoughtful design and dependable code can make the web feel more useful.

I enjoy turning ideas into approachable, easy-to-use websites. My process starts with understanding what a page needs to do, then shaping its structure and visual details around the people who will use it.

I work with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, paying attention to responsive layouts, clear content and the small interactions that make a site feel considered. I’m committed to learning, refining my work and exploring better ways to build for the web.
